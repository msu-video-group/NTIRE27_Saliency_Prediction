Join our next **Video Saliency Prediction Challenge at CVPR 2027** and develop models that predict what captures human attention in video. We will provide **5,000 diverse, high-resolution videos**, with audio-visual information and attention annotations from **more than 19,000 unique observers**.

Building on our [ECCV-AIM 2024](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-91856-8_11) and [CVPR-NTIRE 2026](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026W/NTIRE/papers/Moskalenko_NTIRE_2026_Challenge_on_Video_Saliency_Prediction_Methods_and_Results_CVPRW_2026_paper.pdf) challenges, the 2027 edition invites researchers and practitioners to explore new ideas in visual attention, video modeling, and multimodal learning.

> **The challenge is planned to start in early January 2027.**  
> Star this repository and watch for registration, dataset access, and challenge updates.


## Challenge Task

Develop a **video saliency prediction method** that estimates where viewers focus their attention over time. The challenge is an opportunity to investigate how appearance, motion, temporal context, and sound can inform predictions of human attention.

## Dataset

We will provide a video saliency dataset collected through **crowdsourced mouse tracking** and **validated with conventional eye tracking**.

| Feature | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Videos | **5,000** |
| Unique observers across the dataset | **More than 19,000** |
| Observers per video | **More than 75** |
| Content | Diverse, high-resolution videos |
| Modalities | Audio-visual information |
| Attention annotations | Fixation data collected through crowdsourced mouse tracking |
| Validation | Conventional eye tracking |
| Dataset license | **CC-BY** |

## Evaluation
Submissions will be evaluated using four metrics:

| Metric | Name |
| --- | --- |
| **CC** | Correlation Coefficient |
| **SIM** | Similarity |
| **AUC Judd** | Area Under the ROC Curve, Judd variant |
| **NSS** | Normalized Scanpath Saliency |

### Public Scores and Final Results

During the challenge, participants will see scores computed on **only part of the test set**. **Final evaluation results will remain hidden until the challenge ends.**

This gives participants feedback during development while keeping the final results unrevealed until the competition closes.

The detailed evaluation protocol and submission instructions will be announced later.
